# Financial Invariants and Ledger Accounting

## Equations (transaction invariant)
For each PaidCoinLot: `Available = Granted - Spent - Refunded - Reserved >= 0`. Aggregate: `Wallet.PaidAvailable = Σ Lot.Available`, `Wallet.PaidReserved = Σ Lot.Reserved`, `Wallet.BonusAvailable = Σ Bonus ledger deltas`. `PaidAvailable` EXCLUDES PaidReserved. Every transfer between buckets has ledger legs; no direct Admin balance adjustment.

## Example: unlock 80 Coins, Bonus=30, PaidLots [20,100]
BonusAvailable -30; PaidAvailable -50. Paid allocations: first lot 20, next lot 30. Paid lots' `spent_coins` increment by 20 and 30. `wallet_operations` source unique `ChapterUnlock`, `wallet_entries` Bonus Available -30 and Paid Available -50. In same transaction insert Reading.chapter_unlock and outbox ChapterUnlocked; unique `(user_id,chapter_id)`. The wallet must not be debited twice even if idempotency keys differ.

## Example: complete full 100,000 VND TopUp refund
Original Paid lot granted 10,000; before approval `spent=0`, `refunded=0`, `reserved=0`, lotAvailable=10,000. Approve: lot reserved+10,000; wallet PaidAvailable -10,000, PaidReserved +10,000; append two legs. Externally transfer 95,000 VND after fee 5,000. Verified success: lot reserved−10,000, refunded+10,000; wallet PaidReserved−10,000; append refund completion leg. Verified failed: lot reserved−10,000; wallet PaidReserved−10,000, PaidAvailable+10,000; append reversal legs. Ambiguous payout -> do nothing to reserve.

## Required invariants
- 1 fulfilled TopUp = 1 immutable grant of Paid lot; 1 provider transaction cannot fulfill 2 orders.
- 1 fulfilled VipPurchase = exactly 1 immutable `VipTransaction`, exactly 1 fulfilled provider transaction and exactly 1 VIP-activation outbox message whose identifiers and snapshots agree. `VipSubscription.Version` equals the latest consecutive per-user `SubscriptionVersion`, with no missing/intermediate version and a continuous `PreviousExpiry -> NewExpiry` chain. The complete historical artifact chain is validated fail-closed before renewal.
- A VIP success atomically updates PaymentOrder/provider state, extends the subscription, appends its versioned VIP transaction and writes one activation outbox row. Late, mismatched or integrity-drifted payment evidence creates no entitlement or success outbox. VIP orders never credit Paid Coins; no Bonus Coins refunds.
- Bonus before Paid FIFO and never consume reserved Paid.
- An unlock quote is an immutable five-minute pricing/balance estimate only: creating it cannot mutate Wallet, PaidCoinLot, ledger or entitlement state. It is not a permanent financial ledger row. An expired, unreferenced quote may be deleted only after 30 days from `expires_at`; U02 must protect consumed/referenced quotes and revalidate price, access and balances under its financial locks.
- One successful Chapter unlock atomically creates exactly one immutable User/Chapter entitlement referencing its consumed quote and Wallet operation, Bonus/Paid debit legs, FIFO Paid allocations and one `Reading.ChapterUnlocked.v1` outbox message. Database integrity rejects an entitlement unless the operation is `ChapterUnlock/ReadingUnlock`, its source is that entitlement, its debit-ledger totals equal the persisted Bonus/Paid spend, and its Paid allocations equal Paid spend. The persisted spend and post-operation Wallet snapshots make an idempotent replay deterministic and independent of later mutable Wallet/quote state. A second idempotency key may observe the existing entitlement but can never debit again.
- Refund whole original Paid lot or reject: never substitute a different lot; 7-day request window, 5% fee, one request/order.
- Complete manual refund only with external transfer evidence and reference; outcome ambiguity blocks automatic release/retry.
- Mission grants Bonus at most 300 per VN local event date, without partial grant or carryover. A successful decision atomically updates progress, event-date counter and Wallet, appends exactly one positive Bonus/Available ledger entry bound by a `MissionReward/RewardGrant` operation to the immutable reward grant, writes its success outbox, and marks the receipt processed. A denied-cap decision persists zero financial mutation. Any error rolls the whole receipt transaction back for retry.
- Reconciliation compares balances against ledger/lot components; discrepancy alerts operator, never silently repairs without audited procedure.

## Lock and idempotency
Predetermine lock order across workflows; start with Wallet row for all wallet mutations, then Paid lots in deterministic order. Mission reward processing uses Wallet -> ActionReceipt -> candidate MissionDefinitions ordered by ID -> event-date DailyCounter; this serializes all Bonus grants for one User before applying the 300 cap. A new Chapter unlock continues with the per-User VIP advisory key, quote, Chapter, Comic and ordered Chapter pages. Only after all those decision-bearing locks are held does it sample PostgreSQL `clock_timestamp()` and revalidate expiry, publication/readiness, VIP/pricing and Wallet reconciliation before debit. Committed idempotent results are replayed before mutable validation; the post-Wallet-lock replay check handles an in-flight duplicate safely. Unique User/Chapter, quote and Wallet-operation keys remain the final race barrier. VIP renewal locks the provider identity, per-user subscription/advisory key, subscription row and PaymentOrder in the documented order; concurrent renewals serialize and allocate consecutive subscription versions. Use DB uniqueness for business entitlement/order, persistent operation idempotency for client request replay, provider transaction uniqueness for webhook. Retry safe transient DB failures using same semantic operation key.
